If You Have Insurance, Read This First

With the one exception of PRP, every service on this page — every office visit and every other procedure listed below — is billed to your insurance and covered at $0 out-of-pocket cost to you if you have an accepted plan (Cigna, Anthem Blue Cross, Aetna, United Healthcare, or Medicare PPO). You should never be charged the self-pay prices below if you have applicable insurance.

The self-pay / cash-pay prices on this page apply only to patients without applicable insurance coverage, or who choose to pay directly instead of billing their plan. PRP is a regenerative treatment not covered by any insurance plan, so it is cash-pay for all patients.

Why We Show Self-Pay Pricing

Most patients with an accepted insurance plan pay $0 for everything on this page except PRP — the self-pay prices above only apply to patients without applicable coverage, or who choose to pay directly. A free consultation and a low-cost diagnostic ultrasound keep the front door to care open to everyone regardless of insurance status, and our self-pay procedure pricing is intentionally set below what many corporate clinics in Orange County charge, so patients paying out of pocket still have access to advanced, ultrasound-guided orthobiologic care.

Cash-Pay Pricing & Financial Policy

Insurance is billed by default. With the exception of PRP, which is not covered by any insurance plan, every service listed on this page is eligible for insurance billing. If you have an accepted plan (Cigna, Anthem Blue Cross, Aetna, United Healthcare, or Medicare PPO), we bill your insurance and you owe $0 out of pocket for that service. The self-pay rates listed on this page apply only when you do not have applicable insurance coverage, or when you choose to pay directly instead of billing your plan.

Cash-pay / self-pay rates are separate from insurance billing. The self-pay prices above are separate from, and may differ from, our fee schedules for insurance-based billing, including PPO plans, Medicare PPO, workers’ compensation, and TRICARE. If you elect to pay a self-pay rate for a service instead of billing your insurance, OC Musculoskeletal Medical Center will not submit a claim to your insurance carrier for that service, and you agree to waive your right to submit a claim for reimbursement of that service yourself. Just let our staff know before your visit whether you'd like a service billed to insurance or paid for directly, and we'll confirm your eligibility and coverage.
