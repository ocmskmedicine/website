Why We Offer Cash-Pay Pricing

A free consultation and a low-cost diagnostic ultrasound keep the front door to care open to everyone, regardless of insurance status. Our procedure pricing is intentionally set below what many corporate clinics in Orange County charge, so patients without applicable coverage — or who simply prefer to pay directly — still have access to advanced, ultrasound-guided orthobiologic care.

Cash-Pay Pricing & Financial Policy

Cash-pay / self-pay only. The rates listed on this page apply only to patients who choose to pay directly for their care. They are separate from, and may differ from, our fee schedules for insurance-based billing, including PPO plans, Medicare, workers’ compensation, and TRICARE. Cash-pay pricing is not available to be combined with insurance billing for the same service.

No insurance claims submitted. When you elect to pay a cash-pay rate for a visit or procedure, OC Musculoskeletal Medical Center will not submit a claim to your insurance carrier for that service, and you agree to waive your right to submit a claim for reimbursement of that service yourself. If you would like your insurance billed instead of paying a cash-pay rate, please let our staff know before your visit so we can confirm eligibility and coverage.

Estimate only — not a guarantee of final cost. Prices shown are current as of September 2026 and are subject to change without notice. Your actual cost of care may vary depending on your specific diagnosis, treatment plan, and any additional services your physician recommends (for example, additional visits, imaging, or injections). We will discuss your individualized treatment plan and associated costs with you before any procedure.

Payment terms. Payment is due at the time of service unless other arrangements have been made in advance with our office. We accept major credit cards, debit cards, cash, and check.

No guarantee of outcome. This page is provided for general informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice or a treatment recommendation. All treatment decisions are made on an individual basis following an evaluation by Dr. Salamatipour. As with any medical treatment, individual results vary and no specific outcome is guaranteed.

For additional detail on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. If you have questions about a price, your specific plan, or whether cash-pay is the right option for you, please contact our office — we're happy to help you understand your options before you commit to any treatment.
