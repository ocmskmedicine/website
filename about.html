About Us

Expert Care, Without Surgery.

Every patient at OC Musculoskeletal Medical Center is evaluated and treated by a board-certified sports medicine and family medicine physician with subspecialty fellowship training.

Dr. Ashkan Salamatipour

Sports Medicine & Non-Operative Orthopedic Physician

At OC Musculoskeletal Medical Center, every patient is evaluated and treated by a board-certified sports medicine and family medicine physician with subspecialty fellowship training. Dr. Salamatipour brings a high standard of musculoskeletal care — the kind typically reserved for elite collegiate and professional athletes — to every patient who walks through our door.

During his sports medicine fellowship at Indiana University, Dr. Salamatipour had the privilege of working with Big Ten athletes, including members of the Indiana Hoosiers — the national college football championship team — providing sideline coverage, injury evaluation, and non-operative orthopedic care at the highest level of Division I college athletics. He currently serves as head team physician for the professional American men's soccer team AV Alta FC and USA Boxing.

Every injection, every procedure, and every diagnosis at OCMSK is guided by real-time diagnostic ultrasound, ensuring precision in both identifying and treating the source of pain. This commitment to image-guided care is what sets our clinic apart from conventional orthopedic practice.

Dr. Salamatipour is actively engaged in academic medicine — publishing peer-reviewed research, serving as a journal reviewer, and holding a faculty appointment at the Palmdale Regional Medical Center Family Medicine Residency while precepting medical students across several institutions.
