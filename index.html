About the Clinic

A New Standard in Non-Operative Orthopedic Care


OC Musculoskeletal Medical Center was founded on a simple belief: most orthopedic pain does not require surgery. We offer the most advanced non-operative treatments available, performed with precision ultrasound guidance by a board-certified sports medicine physician.

Every procedure at our clinic is guided by diagnostic ultrasound, ensuring unmatched accuracy in both diagnosis and treatment delivery. This is the same standard of care used in elite professional sports medicine — and it is now the standard for every patient who walks through our door.

Whether you are an active adult managing a nagging injury, an athlete looking to recover without surgery, or someone who continues to experience pain following a surgical or other orthopedic procedure — we have a treatment pathway designed for you.

Treatments & Procedures

Cutting-Edge Therapies,
All Ultrasound-Guided


We offer the most advanced non-operative treatments in orthopedic medicine — the same therapies used with professional and collegiate athletes — performed with diagnostic ultrasound for precision and safety.

01

Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP)

An orthobiologic, regenerative therapy using the newest PRP protocols and technology to concentrate and activate your own platelets and growth factors for faster healing in tendons, ligaments, muscles, and joints. Our protocol also concentrates the regenerative peptide Alpha-2-Macroglobulin (A2M), naturally present in your plasma alongside your platelets.

Learn more ›
02

Viscosupplementation

Viscosupplementation injections that restore joint lubrication, reduce pain, and improve mobility by replenishing hyaluronic acid within the joint space.

03

Prolotherapy

Injection-based treatment using a dextrose solution — combined with ultrasound-guided fenestrations — to stimulate the body's natural repair response in chronically painful ligaments and tendons.

04

Minimally Invasive Tenotomy

A precise needle-based procedure to break up and remove degenerative tendon tissue, promoting healing without open surgery.

05

SPRINT Peripheral Nerve Stimulation (PNS)

A temporary implanted lead delivers mild electrical pulses to peripheral nerves, providing sustained pain relief for chronic musculoskeletal conditions.

06

IOVERA° Cryoneuromodulation

A non-pharmacological neuromodulation technology that uses targeted cold therapy to disrupt pain-signaling nerves — without medication.

07

Ultrasound-Guided Nerve Hydrodissection

A precise injection technique using fluid to release compressed or entrapped peripheral nerves — such as in carpal tunnel, cubital tunnel, and tarsal tunnel syndrome — restoring normal nerve mobility and reducing pain, numbness, and tingling.

Learn more ›
08

Ultrasound-Guided Fascia Hydrodissection

A precise injection technique using fluid — and, for chronic myofascial pain (such as after a whiplash injury), the enzyme hyaluronidase — to release restricted or adhered fascia, restoring normal tissue mobility and reducing pain.

Learn more ›
09

Ultrasound-Guided Capsular Hydrodistension

A minimally invasive procedure that stretches and releases a contracted joint capsule using a carefully injected fluid mixture — commonly used for adhesive capsulitis (frozen shoulder).

Every procedure at OC Musculoskeletal Medical Center is performed under real-time ultrasound guidance. This means diagnoses are confirmed visually before treatment, injections are delivered to the exact target structure, and outcomes are optimized through precision — not approximation.

We are committed to evidence-based medicine. We do not offer unregulated peptide therapies or unproven "stem cell" treatments. Instead, we offer safe, well-studied orthobiologic and regenerative therapies — such as Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP), Hyaluronic Acid (HA), and the regenerative peptide Alpha-2-Macroglobulin (A2M) — that are supported by peer-reviewed research and established clinical use.

Musculoskeletal Conditions,
Managed Without Surgery


We specialize in the non-operative diagnosis and treatment of a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions — using advanced ultrasound guidance to precisely target the source of your pain.

01

Osteoarthritis & Joint Degeneration

Degenerative changes in the knee, hip, shoulder, and other joints causing pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility.

Learn more ›
02

Tendinitis & Chronic Tendinosis

Acute and chronic tendon conditions including rotator cuff tendinopathy, Achilles tendinopathy, patellar tendinopathy, lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow), and De Quervain's tenosynovitis.

Learn more ›
03

Ligament Injuries & Sprains

Ligament tears and chronic ligament laxity affecting the ankle, knee, wrist, and other joints — often successfully managed without surgery.

Learn more ›
04

Peripheral Neuropathy & Nerve Entrapment

Conditions such as carpal tunnel syndrome, cubital tunnel syndrome, tarsal tunnel syndrome, meralgia paresthetica, and other peripheral nerve compression or entrapment syndromes.

Learn more ›
05

Frozen Shoulder (Adhesive Capsulitis)

Progressive shoulder stiffness and pain resulting from thickening and tightening of the joint capsule.

Learn more ›
06

Rotator Cuff Injuries

Rotator cuff tears, rotator cuff tendinopathy, and shoulder impingement syndrome — treated non-operatively with regenerative and ultrasound-guided therapies.

Learn more ›
07

Bursitis

Inflammation of the bursae in the shoulder, hip, knee, elbow, and other joints causing localized pain and swelling.

Learn more ›
08

Plantar Fasciitis & Heel Pain

Chronic heel and arch pain from plantar fascia degeneration, often resistant to conservative measures.

Learn more ›
09

Muscle Injuries & Strains

Acute and chronic muscle tears, strains, and myofascial pain affecting athletes and active individuals.

Learn more ›
10

Chronic Myofascial Pain Syndrome

Chronic trapezius, upper back, and low back pain from thickened, restricted fascia — often following whiplash — treated with ultrasound-guided fascia hydrodissection.

Learn more ›
11

Persistent Pain After Joint Replacement

Ongoing pain following knee, hip, or shoulder replacement surgery, addressed through targeted regenerative and neuromodulation therapies.

12

Trigger Finger (Stenosing Tenosynovitis)

A condition causing a finger to catch or lock when bent, resulting from inflammation of the tendon sheath — treated with precision ultrasound-guided injection.

13

Buttocks, Hip & Low Back Pain

Deep buttock, hip, and SI joint pain from conditions such as piriformis syndrome, gluteal tendinopathy, and sciatic nerve irritation — diagnosed and treated with ultrasound-guided precision.
